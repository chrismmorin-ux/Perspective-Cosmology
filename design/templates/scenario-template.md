# <Scenario title — the situation, not the feature>

## Situation

Where they are, what just happened, what time it is, who is nearby, what they have
already tried. Write it so a simulated walkthrough starts in the right emotional state.

## Their goal in this moment

What they want in this sitting. Often narrower than their overall job: "find out if
this is normal" rather than "manage my health".

## Success looks like

Observable outcomes, from their side. At least one must be something they would tell
a friend about.

## Failure looks like

The outcomes that count as a loss even if they technically finished: they bought the
wrong thing, left feeling judged, or gave data they regret giving.
