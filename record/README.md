# The record

Promoted out of quarantine immediately and on purpose. These documents are
citable. They are the project's own account of where it stood, written largely
before the September 2026 audit and in several places anticipating it.

| File | What it establishes |
|---|---|
| `cd_closure_irreducibility.md` | **CANONICAL.** Proves by explicit countermodel that n_c = 11 follows from *none* of the twenty axioms — n_c = 5 satisfies all of them, partiality included. "We must assume it." |
| `division_algebra_ambiguity_analysis.md` | **CANONICAL.** A blind test in which a different model derived 25 rather than 137 from the same axiom document, with valid arguments, from two counting ambiguities. |
| `DEGREES_OF_FREEDOM_ANALYSIS.md` | The free-parameter accounting behind the "zero free parameters" claim. |
| `STATISTICAL_ANALYSIS_HONEST.md` | The Monte Carlo null that places the framework's integer set at the 51st percentile. |
| `FALSIFIED.md` | The prediction failures, recorded as they happened. |
| `HONEST_ASSESSMENT.md` | The project's own self-evaluation. |

## Why these six and nothing else

They are the files that would survive a hostile reading, because they are the
files that were already hostile to the project. Everything else in the prior
corpus asserts; these measure.

There is a second reason, and it is the one that decided the promotion. This
material is publishable *as it stands*, in a way the physics is not. A
documented case study in how an AI-assisted research loop produces ~700
formulas, relabels assumptions as derivations, and ratchets its own confidence
on internal bookkeeping alone — while recording every step of doing so — is a
contribution to a real and current question. The reference class of
constant-derivation programmes is a century long and almost none of its members
left this kind of trail.

## Reading them

They describe the framework as it was. Nothing here has been updated to reflect
the audit, and it should not be: a record edited after the fact stops being a
record. Where a claim in these documents is now known to be wrong, the
correction belongs in the repository's live content, not in here.
